Pregnancy FAQs

When should I have my first antenatal visit?

Book your first antenatal visit as soon as you know you are pregnant, preferably before 12 weeks.

How many antenatal visits do I need?

WHO recommends at least 8 antenatal care contacts during an uncomplicated pregnancy.

When can I feel my baby's movements?

Most mothers first feel fetal movements between 18 and 20 weeks. In a first pregnancy, it may be closer to 20 weeks.

Is mild abdominal pain normal during pregnancy?

Mild stretching or occasional discomfort can occur. Severe or persistent pain, bleeding, fever or dizziness needs medical assessment.

Can I travel during pregnancy?

Travel is often possible in an uncomplicated pregnancy. Discuss your travel plans with your doctor, especially for long journeys or high-risk pregnancies.

Can I exercise during pregnancy?

Most women with uncomplicated pregnancies can do moderate physical activity. Avoid activities with a high risk of falling or abdominal injury.

Which foods should I avoid during pregnancy?

Avoid alcohol, unpasteurized dairy, raw or undercooked meat and eggs, and food that may be contaminated. Follow local food-safety advice.

Why do I feel nausea and vomiting?

Nausea is common, especially in early pregnancy. Seek medical advice if you cannot keep fluids down or have signs of dehydration.

Do I need iron and calcium supplements?

Iron and folic acid are recommended during pregnancy. Calcium supplementation may also be recommended depending on your diet and local guidelines. Follow your clinician's advice.

What are the warning signs during pregnancy?

Seek urgent medical care for vaginal bleeding, severe abdominal pain, severe headache or blurred vision, leaking fluid, difficulty breathing, or reduced fetal movements after movements are established.

Which tests are important during pregnancy?

Depending on your pregnancy stage and medical history, tests may include blood group, haemoglobin, blood sugar, urine examination, infection screening and ultrasound scans.

When should I have my first ultrasound?

An early ultrasound may help confirm pregnancy location, viability and gestational age. The timing depends on your clinical situation. A dating scan is commonly performed in the first trimester.

Is ultrasound safe during pregnancy?

Medical ultrasound uses sound waves, not ionizing radiation. When performed for appropriate medical reasons by trained professionals, it is considered safe.

How much weight should I gain during pregnancy?

Recommended weight gain depends on your pre-pregnancy BMI and whether you are carrying one baby or multiples. Your doctor can help set an appropriate target.

What is the recommended antenatal care schedule?

WHO recommends 8 antenatal care contacts: before 12 weeks, then around 20, 26, 30, 34, 36, 38 and 40 weeks. Your schedule may need adjustment depending on your health.

Can I sleep on my back during pregnancy?

After 28 weeks, it is advisable to go to sleep on either side rather than on your back. If you wake up on your back, simply turn onto your side.

Is vaginal discharge normal during pregnancy?

Thin, clear or milky-white discharge without an unpleasant smell is common. Seek medical advice if discharge has a bad smell, itching, pain, bleeding or watery leakage.

Can I have sex during pregnancy?

Sex is generally safe in an uncomplicated pregnancy. Avoid it and consult your doctor if you have bleeding, leaking fluid, placenta-related concerns or have been advised to avoid intercourse.

When should I go to the hospital for labour?

Contact your maternity unit when contractions become regular and painful, following the advice given by your doctor. Go immediately for heavy bleeding, reduced fetal movements, leaking fluid or severe pain.

What should I pack in my hospital bag?

Pack your antenatal records, identification documents, comfortable clothes, maternity pads, toiletries, newborn clothes, diapers and essential items for your baby.
